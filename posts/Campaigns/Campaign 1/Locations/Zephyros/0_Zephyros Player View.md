## Character Creation Context

The campaign begins in Zephyros, with characters starting at level 3. Your character may be a resident of Zephyros or a visitor, but they must have a reason to currently be in the city.

A recently exposed crime lord named Mathias Malnor is scheduled to be publicly executed. Public executions are rare in Zephyros and are generally reserved for people whose crimes have affected the city as a whole. Your character should have a reason to attend the execution or be nearby when it occurs. 

The characters do not need to know one another before the campaign begins but they can if that interests anyone.

Y'all know how to write backstories but when writing your backstory, establish:

- Why your character is currently in Zephyros.
- Whether they live in the city and, if so, which district they come from.
- Who in the city do they know
- What is the character trying to accomplish
- One reason they would be willing to work with others when something dangerous happens.

### Major Institutions

- **The [[Eye of Zephyros]]:** The city’s primary police and investigative organization.
- **The [[Hand of Zephyros]]:** Zephyros’ military, security, and intelligence organization.
- **The [[Will of Zephyros]]:** The judicial body responsible for courts, trials, and legal judgments.


# Zephyros

> [!summary] City Summary
> Zephyros is a heavily influential city-state located on the eastern coast of the Sea of Silver. It is best known for its World's Wonder Market, a market open year-round where people from all over eastern Alteres, from master craftsmen to amateur hobbyists, gather to sell their goods. Castle Winterlow lies outside its walls atop Mt. Tempest.

## Public Reputation
Things all citizens know:
- There is a single bridge connecting Northern and Southern Zephyros. 
- The Winterlows came into power after the [[Zephyrian Revolution]] (circa 32 PE)
- Zephyros owes much of its wealth to its ports and the [[World's Wonder Market]], serving as a commercial hub for merchants across Eastern [[Alteres]]. 
- Zephyros operates on a legal code which is enforced by three primary groups: the [[Eye of Zephyros]], the [[Hand of Zephyros]], and the [[Will of Zephyros]].
- Every other year, the city gathers for the [[Winterlow Trials]], a dangerous gauntlet of games and challenges. 
- People who compete in the [[Winterlow Trials]] are held in extremely high regard; the victor is awarded a monetary prize and residence in a mansion in the [[Gilded Quay]] until the next trial.

### Districts

#### [[Government Center]] 
**Purpose:**  
	The Government Center hosts most of Zephyros' government buildings. While the castle lies outside the city walls, the King and those who serve the monarchy visit the Government Center to take care of the city and appeal to citizens' requests.
**Appearance:**  
	Unlike the German/Baroque architecture common in zephyros, the Government Center style mostly resembles Greco-Roman architecture. Sandstone buildings dominate the Government Center. Intricate carvings of past royalty hold pillars of the larger structures with the motif of a meander lining the trim of most buildings
**Who Lives or Works Here:** 
	The majority of workers are monarchy officials, clerks, and civil servants. Besides government buildings, certain shops that are backed/sponsored by the monarchy are set up in the center.

#### [[High District]]
**Purpose:**  
	Residential area for the upper class citizens of Zephyros. Its close proximity to Castle Winterlow and distance from the dense commercial districts makes property here some of the most valuable in the city. Residences vary from single-family houses to large apartments. 
**Appearance:** 
	Well-maintained streets running between half-timbered townhouses, enclosed gardens, and larger private estates. Visually, the architecture in the High District is German, with little Baroque, inspiration.
**Who Lives or Works Here:**  
	Noble families and other highly successful citizen, such as merchants, tradesmen, and retired government officials, live here.


#### [[Squall Ward]]
**Purpose:**  
	The Squall Ward provides inexpensive housing and services for northern Zephyros and houses much of the working class. The district also contains inexpensive inns, workshops, taverns, and neighborhood shops.
**Appearance:**  
	Streets in the Squall Ward are narrow with buildings crowding in and irregular streets. Buildings here are mostly old plaster and weathered timber with patched roofs. Some buildings have new additions attached that are visibly newer. Strong winds and sea spray weather the buildings nearer to the waterfront faster than those more inland.
**Who Lives or Works Here:**  
	Dockworkers, fishers, sailors, laborers, small-scale merchants, and poorer families. 


#### [[Port District]]
**Purpose:**  
	The Port District manages most of the cities port duties. Cargo arriving by sea is unloaded here before being distributed to the rest of the city.
**Appearance:**  
	The waterfront is lined with many ports, piers, warehouses, shipyards, and customs offices. The main roads in the Port District are wide allowing for carts to travel on both sides. Closer to the docks, the sides of roads are often occupied with carts, cargo, and temporary stalls.
**Who Lives or Works Here:**  
	Dockworkers, managers, sailors, and merchants. Many inns and taverns are here to serve the regulars of the district


#### [[Trade District]]
**Purpose:**  
	The Trade District draws merchants, traders, and master craftsmen from all eastern Alteres to the city. The district is famous for the high commercial success of shop keepers who sell here.
**Appearance:**  
	The Trade District surrounds a central marketplace, with wide and stone brick paved roads. Permanent shops occupy the bottom floors of buildings with temporary stalls lining along the roads. The shops in this district sell a diverse expanse of specialized goods.
**Who Lives or Works Here:**  
	The majority of buildings in the Trade Ward are markets, inns, or taverns. Some shop owners live in residences above or below their stores.

#### [[Bridge Ward]]
**Purpose:**  
	The Bridge Ward surrounds the southern approach to the only bridge connecting northern and southern Zephyros. It serves travelers, transports goods between both halves of the city, and acts as a meeting point between several districts.
**Appearance:**  
	The streets of the Bridge Ward are dense and heavily traveled, with major roads converging near the bridge. Coaching inns, stables, warehouses, taverns, and mixed-use buildings line the principal roads, while quieter residential streets fill the spaces between them.
**Who Lives or Works Here:**  
	Cart drivers, stablehands, innkeepers, guards, messengers, ferrymen, and small merchants are common. Its central location also attracts residents who work elsewhere in the city.


#### [[Eastgate]]
**Purpose:**  
	The Eastgate serves as the main point of entry for travelers and goods arriving overland. It provides services for caravans before they enter the more crowded center of Zephyros. It also provides temporary housing for travelers coming from far away.
**Appearance:**  
	The district is less densely built than the rest of southern Zephyros. Large courtyards, stables, storage yards, workshops, warehouses, and roadside inns stand along the roads leading from its fortified entry gates.
**Who Lives or Works Here:**  
	Caravan merchants, animal handlers, guards, farmers, craftspeople, and travelers are common. Many residents make their living supplying or repairing wagons before they continue into or out of the city.

#### [[Gilded Quay]]
**Purpose:**  
	The Gilded Quay hosts many affluent waterfront estates and mansions. Properties in the Gilded Quay are often reserved for members of the royal families, wealthy aristocrats, and foreign representatives. The ports in the Gilded Quay are private access for residents only The district also has one property reserved for the most recent winner of the [[Winterlow Trials]]. 
**Appearance:**  
	Grand stone residences and ornate commercial buildings overlook private piers and landscaped courtyards. Intricate facades and alabaster sculptures decorate the carefully maintained streets.
**Who Lives or Works Here:**  
	Merchant families, diplomats, financiers, visiting dignitaries, and other wealthy residents live here. Their households employ servants, guards, interpreters, and specialist craftspeople.

#### [[Hallowed Ward]]
**Purpose:**  
	The Hallowed Ward houses all of Zephyros' funerary service, such as cemeteries/burial grounds. Many houses of worship also have buildings in the district in close proximity to the burial grounds. 
**Appearance:**  
	The district is quieter and more open than the surrounding wards. Temples, shrines, mausoleums, gardens, and tree-lined paths occupy much of its land, with residences and workshops concentrated along its outer roads.
**Who Lives or Works Here:**  
	Clergy, groundskeepers, healers, undertakers, scribes, and craftspeople who produce religious or funerary goods work here. Very few people permanently live in the Hallowed Ward, though visitors are common
