# Soul

Soul is the father of all creation in [[Drakensveil]]. Soul's being is split into four tempers, which in combination are used to provide souls to the living. The four tempers are Woe, Frolic, Dread, and Malice.

At the dawn of time, Soul harness the volatile energy in the [[Zero Plane]] to create 12 children split into 4 triads, each representing one of Soul's tempers.
