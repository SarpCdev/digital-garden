
# The Eternal Ones

The Eternal Ones is a pantheon of celestials born to [[Soul]] responsible for the creation of the 12 material planes in Drakensveil. The pantheon is divided into the four Perfect Triads, each resembling one of the four tempers of [[Soul]].
